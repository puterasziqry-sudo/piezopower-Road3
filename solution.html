02 / SOLUTION

A road system built around
harvest → store → use.

PiezoPower Road connects piezoelectric harvesting, electrical conditioning, battery storage and LED lighting into one energy cycle.

The solution

PiezoPower Road turns daytime traffic pressure into stored electrical energy.

Our solution uses protected piezoelectric materials beneath a traffic-bearing road surface. The generated electrical output is conditioned and stored in a battery, then supplied to LED street lighting after sunset.

01 / INPUT

Vehicle pressure

Moving vehicles load the road surface.

02 / CONVERSION

Piezoelectric layer

Mechanical strain generates electrical charge.

03 / STORAGE

Battery system

Conditioned electricity is accumulated during the day.

04 / OUTPUT

LED lighting

Stored energy is used at night.

Prototype architecture

One system. Two operating periods.

The prototype visualises the complete pathway: road surface, piezoelectric material, electrical circuit, energy storage and LED street light.

Important: the prototype represents the proposed architecture. Actual road-scale energy yield must be validated experimentally for the chosen materials, traffic load and site.
Applications

Designed for targeted, low-power infrastructure.

LED street lighting

Primary proposed application in this project: use stored daytime energy after sunset.

Roadside sensors

Potential future use for selected low-power sensing and monitoring systems.

Smart-road infrastructure

Could complement other energy and monitoring technologies in a wider smart-road system.
